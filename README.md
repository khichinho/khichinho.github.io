### 🎯 What Sets Me Apart

I don't just build ML systems—I architect them to be **vendor-agnostic by design**. 

Whether it's replacing proprietary blob storage with MinIO/LakeFS or architecting platforms that run identically on EKS and bare-metal Kubernetes, I prioritize **portability over convenience**. This matters when you're serving Fortune 500 clients who need multi-cloud flexibility.

---

### 🛠 Engineering Principles

My approach to building production systems focuses on sovereignty and reproducibility:

* **Sovereignty over Convenience:** Prioritizing open standards (S3 API, OCI Containers) over proprietary cloud services to ensure long-term portability and cost arbitrage.
* **Platform as a Product:** Building self-service Internal Developer Platforms (IDPs) that abstract complexity, allowing data scientists to ship models without managing infrastructure.
* **Immutable MLOps:** Enforcing strict versioning for data, code, and model artifacts to guarantee auditability in regulated industries like Automotive and Finance.
